DATA STORIES / 01 · MONETARY POLICY

How quickly do mortgage rates follow monetary policy?

Policy rates can move overnight. Mortgage pricing is a different story. Explore how the two have evolved in Czechia—and why the gap between them is not a bank profit margin.

02 / UNDERSTAND

Why don't the two rates move together?

The CNB's two-week repo rate is the main monetary-policy instrument. It affects very short-term funding conditions, while mortgage offers—often fixed for several years—reflect expectations about rates over a longer horizon, banks' funding costs, credit risk, competition and the characteristics of new lending.

A visual lag is not an estimated causal lag. The graph compares two time series with different economic meanings. It does not identify a monetary-policy shock or estimate the transmission coefficient. Nor is the arithmetic gap between the two lines a measure of banks' margins.

For a more direct look at mortgage funding costs, compare mortgage rates with an appropriate interest-rate swap (IRS) maturity in a follow-up analysis.

03 / REPRODUCE

From chart to reproducible analysis.

The full companion R script uses aradR to retrieve the selected ARAD mortgage indicator, inspects its metadata, and combines it with the official CNB policy-rate history. You will need a personal ARAD API key. The interactive chart itself uses the corresponding public ECB MIR series, so visitors need no credentials.

R · aradR
install.packages("aradR")
library(aradR)

# Use your personal ARAD API key
# ARAD_API_KEY is read from ~/.Renviron

# Inspect the mortgage-rate series metadata in ARAD:
info <- arad_info("SMIRNOOBUVMIRS305CZK011111")

mortgage <- arad_get(
  indicator_ids = "SMIRNOOBUVMIRS305CZK011111",
  from = "2015-01-01"
)

04 / DATA & METHODS

Definitions matter.

Mortgage series
New business, loans to households for house purchase, Czech koruna, total across initial rate fixation periods. Annualised agreed rate / narrowly defined effective rate, percent per annum. Monthly frequency. Corresponding ARAD indicator SMIRNOOBUVMIRS305CZK011111. The public chart is populated from ECB MIR series MIR.M.CZ.B.A2C.A.R.A.2250.CZK.N, based on Czech banking statistics.
Policy rate
CNB two-week repo rate, recorded as the rate in effect on the last calendar day of each month, percent per annum. This is a month-end observation, not the monthly average.
Coverage and updates
2015 onward; the latest month is the latest available mortgage observation. Data are retrieved during website generation, so this is not a live intraday feed. The latest mortgage observation may be preliminary and subsequently revised.
Interpretation
Descriptive comparison, not a causal identification of policy transmission. Differences in definitions, timing and horizons are material.
